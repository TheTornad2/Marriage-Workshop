What You'll Learn

Transform your marriage through faith-based principles and practical tools

Newly marriages might be scary, but you're not alone. You have us.

This workshop is specifically designed for young couples who want to build stronger marriages through Christlike communication, emotional intimacy, and peaceful conflict resolution.

Each 45-minute session blends gospel principles with research-based relationship skills that you can apply immediately in your home. Join other couples on this journey of growth and strengthen your eternal partnership.

Workshop Sessions

Each session builds on the previous one to create lasting change

01

Building Christlike Communication

What You'll Discover:

  • Three major communication barriers that weaken relationships
  • The difference between reactive and intentional communication
  • How to speak words that edify and strengthen (Ephesians 4:29)
  • Active listening techniques that build connection

Interactive Activities:

  • Real-life story: "When We Learned to Listen"
  • Role-play: Communication Barriers vs. Christlike Communication
  • Group discussion on breaking negative patterns
02

Charity and Emotional Intimacy

What You'll Discover:

  • How charity—the pure love of Christ—strengthens marriage
  • Practical ways to practice patience and empathy daily
  • Understanding and meeting emotional needs
  • Small acts of love that create lasting bonds

Interactive Activities:

  • Scripture reflection on The Family Proclamation
  • Video: "Your Spouse Is Not Your Enemy"
  • "Love in Action" commitment activity
03

Peaceful and Spirit-Guided Conflict Resolution

What You'll Discover:

  • The "Pause, Pray, and Proceed" method
  • Using "I" statements instead of blame
  • Focusing on problems, not attacking the person
  • Forgiveness as a healing practice (Matthew 18:21-22)

Interactive Activities:

  • Conflict resolution role-plays
  • Scripture study on forgiveness
  • Creating your couple's conflict resolution plan
